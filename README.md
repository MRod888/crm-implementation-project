# Salesforce CRM Implementation Project

### IT Project Management | Business Analysis | Software Implementation

## Project Overview

This project is a simulated Salesforce CRM implementation for Apex Solutions, a fictional company with 100 employees. The objective is to demonstrate the planning, coordination, and business analysis skills required to successfully manage an enterprise software implementation.

The project follows a structured 12-week implementation plan with a hypothetical budget of $75,000. It covers the major phases of the project management lifecycle, including initiation, planning, execution, monitoring, and closure.

Through this simulation, I will develop project management documentation, analyze business requirements, identify potential risks, and demonstrate how an IT project coordinator would manage a software implementation.

## Business Problem

Apex Solutions currently relies on spreadsheets, email communication, and disconnected systems to manage customer information and sales activities.

This creates several operational challenges:

- Duplicate and inconsistent customer records
- Limited visibility into sales pipeline performance
- Inefficient communication between departments
- Difficulty tracking customer interactions and follow-ups
- Time-consuming manual reporting processes

The proposed Salesforce implementation aims to centralize customer information, improve operational efficiency, and provide better visibility into sales and customer service performance.

## Project Objectives

The primary objectives of this project are to:

1. Develop a comprehensive implementation plan for Salesforce CRM within a 12-week timeframe.
2. Maintain a hypothetical project budget of $75,000.
3. Plan the migration of existing customer data into a centralized CRM platform.
4. Establish training and onboarding procedures for 30 employees.
5. Identify and mitigate potential project risks.
6. Develop a stakeholder communication and project monitoring strategy.
7. Define measurable success criteria, including a target of 90% active user adoption within 30 days of launch.

## Project Scope

**In Scope**
- CRM requirements gathering and software configuration planning
- Customer data migration planning
- Stakeholder identification and communication
- Project scheduling and resource allocation
- Budget development and cost tracking
- Risk assessment and mitigation
- User acceptance testing and employee training plans
- Deployment and post-implementation support planning

**Out of Scope**
- Development of a custom CRM platform
- Replacement of existing company hardware
- Implementation of unrelated business systems
- Actual deployment of Salesforce in a production environment
